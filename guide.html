    5. USB Audio, Bluetooth MIDI & Firmware Flashing

    Routing rules for DAW recording, external Bluetooth MIDI keyboards, and updating or restoring M-VAVE FM-1 hardware.

    Audio & MIDI Routing (GLO 5/5 & REC)

    • USB Audio Independence: The physical speaker/headphone amplifier level is controlled on GLO 5/5 → K4 (SPK/HP), including an OFF setting for silent USB-audio monitoring into Ableton or another DAW at full 44.1 kHz line level.
    • USB Synth + Drum Mix: HOME 3/3 balances global SYNTH LVL, KICK DUCK (sidechain compression depth), DUCK SRC (Kick / Kick+Snare / All), and DRM LVL.
    • Bluetooth MIDI (REC button): Short-press REC to open the BLE scan/connect list for wireless keyboards or computers; hold REC for 1 second to toggle Bluetooth radio power.
    • Hardware Combos & Easter Eggs: Press OCT- + OCT+ together for the STARFISH About App page & Chorusy Cmaj9 shimmer; hold HOME + SEL for A/B Patch Compare; hold GLO + ARP for Disco Starfish; hold ARP + OCT+/- for Arp Latch.
    • Channel Assignment: GLO 5/5 sets Synth RX/TX MIDI channel (K1), Drum channel (K2), and BLE mode (K3: Off / Computer / External Keyboard).

    Installing Firmware to FM-1 Hardware

    • Step 1 — Export & Build: Push the firmware source (from Export GitHub .zip or this repository) to GitHub. GitHub Actions automatically compiles starfish.fwsc and publishes Releases + GitHub Pages (index.html + guide.html).
    • Step 2 — Open Web Installer: Open the STARFISH Web MIDI Installer in Chrome or Edge with your FM-1 connected directly via a USB-C data cable.
    • Step 3 — Flash Safely: The page auto-loads starfish.fwsc (or lets you drop a downloaded .fwsc file). Confirm the backup prompt, press Install, and wait until the status reaches Done.
    • Recovery & Stock Return: The same Web Installer includes a verified stock M-VAVE firmware restore workflow and hardware crash-log reader.
