# STARFISH

STARFISH is an 8-voice virtual analog synthesizer, 10-kit synthesized drum machine, arpeggiator, and multi-FX firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on `fm1-base`.

![STARFISH Home screen](manual/img/screen.png "STARFISH on the FM-1 screen")

Version {{VERSION}}. You install it at your own risk, and the web installer can restore M-VAVE's stock firmware at any time.

- [Install STARFISH](install/) directly over USB MIDI in Chrome or Edge.
- [Try it in the browser](emu/) using WebAssembly and WebAudio.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}) for manual or offline flashing.
- Read the [Manual](manual/) for panel controls, drum sequencing, and Bank X saving.

Built on [fm1-base](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-base) and felucca-bsp (MPL-2.0). M-VAVE and FM-1 are trademarks of their respective owners.
